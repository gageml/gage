Noteworthy finding in a session, from a roadmap scan

An observation of agent behavior or user friction, written by an agent that
read an abbreviated roadmap of one full session and selectively retrieved
full message text where the roadmap looked relevant. The value is a
description of the observation. The target is the session. `lines` metadata,
when present, gives the session lines the observation refers to.

The writer sees the whole session in abbreviated form and skips problems the
session itself diagnosed and fixed. Content elided by the abbreviation was
not seen unless the writer retrieved it. It has no visibility across
sessions.
